## Changes made
- Room 01 and 06 now have actual open-bottom inverted-U frames, appropriate offsets/tilt, wider low plinths and the final frame's inner light strips.
- Room 02 uses curved lenticular leaves, thin branching tubes and suspended wires over an irregular rock, with a real ceiling well.
- Room 03 no longer uses the unsuccessful hanging square cloth. It has separately modeled crown/jaw veil, hood, solid copper chest/neck and overlapping wrapped surfaces. These still lack the concept's sculpted anatomical and drapery detail.
- Room 04 has three broad orbital bands, thin supports, hanging rectangular pendulums with disc weights and a gold particle projection.
- Room 05 uses 32 narrow curved metal ribs rather than filled planar panels, with two crests and a valley.
- Concrete wall thickness, foreground entrance partition and final light opening have real geometry. The physical six-room circuit still differs from the separate concept compositions.
- Reconstructed artwork textures are shared by the gallery and zoom dialogs via paintingUrl. New locally generated stone, bronze and rock albedo maps are used. Ground contact AO and a softened desaturated planar reflection are present.
- Starting camera positions and wall collision geometry were reconciled after the new framing initially prevented walking.
